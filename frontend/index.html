Our Story

Ajab is a prominent brand of fortified wheat and maize flours in Kenya, produced by Grain Industries Limited (GIL), known for high-quality, toxin-safe products used for baking and cooking. It is popular for making chapati, mandazi, and bread.

Founded in the early 2000s, Ajab Flour has grown to become a trusted name in households and businesses across Africa. With a commitment to quality and nutrition, we have expanded our product range to include maize flour, millet flour, self-raising flour, and more.

Our mission is simple: Provide affordable, nutritious, and high-quality flours to every African kitchen. We believe that good nutrition starts with quality ingredients, and we're committed to being part of Africa's food security solution.
